life-os steps: privacy policy

life-os steps is a personal app, used only by its owner, Marcus Ong. It has no other users.

What it reads. With the owner's permission, it reads his own daily step counts from the Google Health API, read-only (the googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly permission). It reads no other data.

Where the data goes. The step counts are stored only on the owner's own computer, for his personal food and fitness log. They are not shared with anyone, not sold, not used for advertising, and not sent to any other service.

Removing access. The permission can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can read nothing more.

Google user data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
